# Faithocity — Church sample sites (Brady)

From James Brady in SaaS chat — **reference for features**, not MVP complexity (custom WordPress).

| Site | URL | Patterns noted |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Second Calvary | https://2cal.org/ | Events, mission/vision, pastor, ways to give, live worship |
| Central Baptist (Baltimore) | https://thecentralbaptistchurch.org/ | Watch latest sermon, upcoming events, guiding principles |
| Ray of Hope | https://www.rayofhope.org/ | Pastor highlight, events slider, ministries, member services |
| Clay Street Baptist | https://csmbc.org/ | Pastor, service times, events, sermon, youth ministry |

**MVP takeaway:** Always-on patterns → Home, Pastor, Ministries, Events, Give (outbound), Watch Live (YouTube/link). Keep modules simple vs these full custom builds.
